## Claude

Four cat puzzles: a 5×5 kitten, two 10×10s (loaf, face), and a 15×15 sitting cat with a curled tail.

On the images — I hand-drew the pixel grids rather than downsampling photos. Real photos threshold into noise at 10×10 and produce clue sets that aren't logically solvable, so nonogram art is always authored. The grids live in the `PUZZLES` array as `#`/`.` strings, so adding your own cat is a matter of appending 10 or 15 strings; clues are derived at load.

A few mechanics worth knowing:
- Win detection compares your clues against the puzzle's clues, not against the stored grid, so alternate valid solutions still count.
- Drag paints a run; the first cell decides whether the stroke fills or erases.
- Completed rows/columns dim their clue numbers.
- Hint fills one correct square and adds 30 seconds.
- Best times save through `window.storage` when it exists, and degrade silently to in-memory if you open the file straight from disk.
